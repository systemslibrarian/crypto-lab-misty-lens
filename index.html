Misty Lens

MISTY1 · KASUMI · Related-Key

Run MISTY1 and KASUMI side by side, inspect the parts changed for cellular hardware, and execute the seven-round distinguisher those changes enabled.

WHY IT MATTERS

A security argument belongs to one exact design, not to its name or round count. Simplifying a proven structure can silently remove the assumptions that made its bound hold.

One ancestor, two security stories

MISTY1 was designed so its nested structure supported lower bounds against linear and differential cryptanalysis. KASUMI adapted that design for cellular hardware, keeping eight rounds while changing the key schedule and inner functions. This lab shows why those are not interchangeable claims.
